Corn to Ethanol

Cultivating Carbon Clarity

Agricultural carbon data is a maze of terrifying spreadsheets, dense scientific metrics, and fragmented pipelines. We built a unified emissions dashboard for Gevo that takes the headache out of sustainability tracking. By translating complex environmental science into an intuitive, real-time interface, we empowered everyday farmers and corporate ethanol producers to track, verify, and report their green metrics with absolute confidence—and unlock massive premium market incentives.

  • RoleLead Product Designer
  • CompanyGevo
  • IndustrySustainability / Carbon Markets
  • Year2023–2024
  • TimelineAug – Feb
01 / The Problem

Lost in the Muddy Data

Before this dashboard, calculating carbon impact felt less like modern tech and more like a second full-time job for everyone involved:

The Business Bottleneck

Gevo needed to track emissions across the entire supply chain—from a single acre of corn to thousands of gallons of ethanol. Without a central hub, farmers couldn't prove their low-carbon practices, locking them out of lucrative premium markets and green tax credits.

The Manual Grind

Workflows were trapped in the 1990s—relying on endless phone calls, clunky spreadsheets, and manual entry. It was slow, error-prone, and a total nightmare for a farmer who just spent a 12-hour day in a tractor.

The Cognitive Gap

The underlying systems were fragmented across different regions, forcing a brutal UI challenge: how do you design an interface that gives carbon scientists the hyper-precise math they need, while remaining simple enough for a non-technical farmer to navigate on an iPad?

Why it mattered

Numbers alone don’t drive action. By bridging the gap between raw field data and corporate compliance, we could turn sustainable farming into a provable, profitable economic engine.

Learning sessions with farmers and ethanol producers

02 / Constraints

The High-Stakes MVP

We had to plant, grow, and harvest this platform under a tight regulatory window:

The 7-Month Horizon

We had to go from absolute scratch to a regulatory-compliant, production-ready MVP in just seven months.

A Polarized Audience

We were designing for polar-opposite user personas—low-digital-literacy farmers looking for quick, high-level summaries vs. data scientists demanding granular calculation validation.

Lean Engineering

A small development crew meant every complex interactive chart or data pipeline visualization had to be engineered with extreme structural efficiency.

Mapping how ethanol producers provide their data

03 / Strategy

Visual Storytelling Over Raw Math

Our north-star experience principle was clarity over complexity. We staked our design on a core hypothesis:

Old way

Scan 50 spreadsheet rows

New way

See the green trend line, download the report

If we leaned into high-level visual storytelling (showing macro trends and simple color-coded health indicators) instead of overwhelming users with dense tables, farmers would spend less time wrestling with data and ethanol producers would instantly gain trust in their suppliers' transparency.

Design facilitation on the MVP platform IA

04 / UX Work

Field Research and Fact-Finding

We didn't design this from the comfort of an office chair. We conducted extensive learning sessions with farmers, ethanol plant managers, and environmental scientists to figure out where the data pipelines were breaking down.

We used core usability heuristics to transition our findings into low- and mid-fidelity wireframes, mapping out complex interactive scenarios. Our biggest breakthrough came during SME feedback: testing proved that showing a dynamic, contextual visual trend timeline built instant comprehension, while raw data blocks left users second-guessing their compliance standing.

Mapping the data team's stewarding system for farmers

05 / UI Design

Designing a Trustworthy System

To ensure a tiny dev team could build this at lightning speed, we constructed a robust, data-driven design language directly into Gevo’s digital ecosystem:

The Visual Language

Clean typography with ample white space, a trustworthy color palette (subtle earthy neutrals paired with accessible, vibrant semantic greens and blues), and crisp, responsive layout systems.

The Modular Components

A highly reusable library of flexible filter menus, interactive data charts, clean report summary cards, and clear submission states.

Production Readiness

Full spacing, color, and typographic token definitions paired with live Figma annotations ensured the frontend team could ship dynamic charts and complex loading states with near-zero friction.

First release of the design system

06 / Mission Control

Cross-Functional Alignment

With high-stakes compliance data, cross-functional alignment was everything. We partnered closely with the CPO, lead engineers, and agricultural data experts on a daily basis.

We brought a hyper-realistic interactive prototype with real-world dummy data to stakeholders and investors early. This near-live preview allowed us to stress-test data loading states and edge cases with engineers while ensuring corporate alignment on compliance goals before a single line of production code was written.

Screenshots of the prototype

07 / The Payload

Seamless Sustainability

The resulting dashboard didn't just look modern—it completely overhauled how emissions data moves through the agricultural supply chain:

Time Saved

Farmers and ethanol plant managers saw a massive 35% reduction in the time it took to prepare and verify compliance reports.

Instant Verification

Corporate producers gained total, uninhibited clarity into their upstream supply chain emissions, erasing manual validation delays.

Proven Scalability

The beta program was such a functional proof-of-concept for green compliance that company leadership immediately tapped the framework to be scaled across multiple other renewable fuel initiatives.

The ultimate takeaway

Numbers don't change behavior; context does. When you give users a tangible sense of control over their data through smart visual storytelling, you bridge the gap between environmental responsibility and real economic reward.
