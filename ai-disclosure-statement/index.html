AI disclosure statement

Human-Crafted. AI-Assisted.

In an industry rapidly adopting automation, I believe the core of great design remains stubbornly human. I treat AI as a powerful technical tool in my stack—no different than a game engine or a design suite—but it is never the author of my work.

The Human Core (where AI is locked out)

  • Visual & UI design: Every pixel, layout, and visual hierarchy is hand-crafted.
  • 3D modeling & illustration: All geometry, textures, and custom illustrations are built by me.
  • HTML & CSS coding: The foundational markup and styling that brings designs to life on the web are human-written.
  • Design system architecture: The structural decisions that scale a product require human foresight, empathy, and intent.
  • Ideation, brainstorming & curation: The initial spark, conceptual exploration, and creative decision-making are entirely mine. I do not outsource my understanding of a problem.
  • Note taking & synthesis: I process my own notes to ensure a deep, authentic grasp of the project.
  • Final asset production: All deliverables are human-made, carrying a strict anti-scraping and anti-AI training policy.

The Assistant (where I leverage AI)

  • Research & documentation: Scanning large volumes of technical documentation, engine specs, or market trends.
  • Technical learning: Acting as an interactive tutor to help me break down complex code mechanics or learn new frameworks faster.
  • Code reviews & troubleshooting: Spotting syntax errors or helping optimize logic in complex scripts.
  • Reviewing writing: Proofreading my human-written text for grammar, clarity, and conciseness.

Holistic Design (60% human / 40% AI)

I approach my overall workflow with a 60/40 methodology. I leverage AI to handle the foundational, structural busywork (the 40%), reserving the dominant majority of the process (the 60%) strictly for pure human creative direction, craft, and curation.

  • Rapid prototyping: Accelerating the path from high-fidelity UI to an interactive proof-of-concept.
  • Foundational scaffolding: Using AI to lay out boilerplate and initial code frameworks, freeing up my energy for the critical human polish.
  • Stress-testing UI: Generating realistic placeholder data (names, inventory items, edge cases) to see how my UI systems handle dynamic content.
